Delete Your AI Reminder Account

We respect your privacy. If you no longer want to use AI Reminder, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support email address.
  2. Use the subject: AI Reminder - Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address registered with your AI Reminder account.
  4. Our support team will verify your request and process it.
Request Account Deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • Your registered account information.
  • Your saved reminders and descriptions.
  • Associated personal data stored in our Supabase database.

Data Retention

We will delete applicable account information and associated data within 30 days after verifying a valid deletion request. Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Backup copies may remain temporarily until securely overwritten according to our backup retention process.

Will Your Account Be Deleted Immediately?

No. Account deletion requests are verified before processing. We will notify you when your deletion request has been completed.

Important: Uninstalling the AI Reminder app does not delete your account or cloud-stored data. You must submit an account deletion request.
