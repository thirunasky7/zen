1. Introduction

AI Reminder respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the AI Reminder mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address used to register and sign in.
  • Account identifier generated by our authentication provider.
  • Authentication and account-security information necessary to verify your account.

Reminder information

  • Reminder titles and descriptions that you enter.
  • Reminder dates, times, categories, and preferences.
  • Other information you choose to save in the application.

Device and advertising information

Google AdMob and its SDK may process information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, app interactions, advertisement interactions, and diagnostic information. The information processed depends on the SDK version, app configuration, device settings, and applicable consent choices.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate sign-ins.
  • Create, store, and synchronize reminders.
  • Provide app features and reminder notifications.
  • Maintain app security and prevent misuse or fraud.
  • Understand errors and improve app performance.
  • Display and measure advertisements through Google AdMob.

4. Supabase Cloud Services

AI Reminder uses Supabase for cloud database and authentication services. Account information and reminder information may be transmitted to and stored using Supabase to provide sign-in, data storage, and synchronization features.

Supabase privacy information: https://supabase.com/privacy.

5. Google AdMob Advertisements

AI Reminder uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its SDK may process information such as IP address, advertising identifiers, device information, app interactions, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related services.

Whether advertisements are personalized may depend on your settings, consent, location, and applicable requirements.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
AdMob Help: https://support.google.com/admob/.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app:

  • Supabase: cloud database and authentication services.
  • Google AdMob: advertising delivery, measurement, and related services.

These providers process information under their own applicable privacy policies and service terms. Their processing may include uses described in those policies.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. These may include secure network connections, access controls, and backend security measures. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your AI Reminder account and associated personal information by emailing thirunasky7@gmail.com.

Please use the subject AI Reminder – Account Deletion Request and include the email address registered with your account. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

After verifying a request, we will delete the account and associated personal information, including reminder information stored in our systems, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. Information that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes will be retained only as permitted or required. Backup copies may remain temporarily under our backup retention procedures.

Important: An account deletion request must be processed even if you no longer have the app installed. Contact us using the email address above if you cannot access the in-app deletion option.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the app, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and meet applicable legal obligations. Following a verified deletion request, personal information will be deleted from active systems, subject to permitted legal retention and temporary backup retention.

10. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization where required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.
